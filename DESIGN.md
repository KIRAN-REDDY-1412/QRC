# Design System: SmartQR Menu

## Philosophy & Point of View
SmartQR Menu is designed for Indian dining establishments, bridging casual dhabas and street bistros with royal Awadhi palaces and 5-star luxury lounges. The design rejects the generic "one-size-fits-all" digital menu paradigm in favor of 15 handcrafted, culturally grounded visual layouts.

## Typography
- **Primary Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700, 800)
- **Classical & Editorial Serifs**: `Playfair Display`, serif (Weights: 500, 600, 700)
- **Royal Heritage & Fine Dining**: `Cinzel`, serif (Weights: 600, 700, 800, 900)
- **Modern Displays**: `Outfit`, sans-serif
- **Data & Pricing**: Monospaced tabular numerals for prices and transaction receipts

## Color Strategy: Committed & High Contrast
- **Backgrounds**: Slate 950 (`#020617`), Slate 900 (`#0f172a`), Obsidian (`#08090b`), Warm Cream (`#fcf9f2`)
- **Primary Brand**: Emerald 600 (`#059669`), Emerald 500 (`#10b981`), Emerald 400 (`#34d399`)
- **Gold & Royal Accents**: Gold 500 (`#f59e0b`), Saffron 500 (`#d97706`), Gold Metallic Foil (`#d4af37`)
- **Dietary Standards (Indian FSSAI)**:
  - Vegetarian: Green square with solid green circle (`#16a34a`)
  - Non-Vegetarian: Brown/Red square with solid triangle (`#dc2626`)
  - Contains Egg: Amber square with yellow circle (`#d97706`)

## 15 Distinct Template Visual Archetypes
1. **Classic Menu** (Free): Traditional printed restaurant board, double-column on desktop, dotted price leaders.
2. **Simple Restaurant** (Free): Streamlined single-column list, high readability, fast-casual focus.
3. **Basic Modern** (Free): Contemporary card grid with balanced whitespace and rounded category pills.
4. **Modern Food Gallery** (Premium): Photography-first layout with high-impact hero dish cards and touch swipe.
5. **Elegant Restaurant** (Premium): Editorial warm cream and deep slate palette with Playfair Display serifs.
6. **Minimal White** (Premium): Scandinavian architectural minimalism, stark monochrome contrast, micro-typography.
7. **Dark Food Menu** (Premium): Nocturnal lounge aesthetic with emerald/amber neon accents on true dark grounds.
8. **Colorful Cafe** (Premium): Playful pastel tones, bubbly pill badges, and cheery brunch atmosphere.
9. **Premium Dining** (Premium): Structured double-column cards, chef recommendation crests, and spice meters.
10. **Luxury Black Gold** (Premium Plus): Obsidian velvet tone, brushed metallic gold foil trim, Cinzel roman typography.
11. **Fine Dining** (Premium Plus): Haute cuisine degustation presentation with sommelier wine pairing notes.
12. **Royal Indian Heritage** (Premium Plus): Maharaja palace design with heritage jali patterns, brass, and saffron accents.
13. **Glassmorphism Menu** (Premium Plus): Frosted glass translucency with backdrop blur and dynamic lighting glows.
14. **Advanced Food Showcase** (Premium Plus): Interactive culinary lab with expandable nutrition macros and prep times.
15. **Exclusive Signature** (Premium Plus): Bespoke celebrity private dining aesthetic with dark velvet and platinum trim.

## Quality Standards
- Zero gradient text on body or titles
- Touch targets >= 44x44px for category tabs and filters
- No-app-download zero friction diner experience
- Permanent QR links that never invalidate upon menu or tier changes
